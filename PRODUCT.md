## Stack

[2026-09-23: the Vite/React SPA has been replaced — this is now the shipped stack, not a plan.] Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19, plain JS/JSX (no TypeScript), plain CSS, split into per-component CSS Modules (`app/globals.css` holds tokens, reset and base type only). Fonts via `next/font/google`: Unbounded (display) + JetBrains Mono (body/labels). Animation/scroll: [2026-09-24, 0.2.0; 0.2.1: desktop only, touch devices scroll natively] Lenis smooths wheel scrolling and the scroll layer publishes one progress value the scene reads; [2026-09-30, 0.2.8] GSAP, installed for later choreography but never shipped, was removed. The background is a WebGL2 renderer of the studio's MIST scene (`components/gradient/gl/`), driven by per-theme recipe files under `components/gradient/recipes/`. The generated SVG engine it was ported from (`components/gradient/engine.js`) was the fallback for browsers without WebGL until 0.1.8, when a layered DOM renderer (`components/gradient/layers/`) replaced it and the engine was deleted. [2026-09-24: WebGL became the default in 0.1.4.] The earlier ambient experiments (a Three.js "LiquidMesh" and a 2D particle field) were deleted, and `three` has been removed. Deployed on Vercel [2026-09-24]; [2026-09-30] on the custom domain abgupta.me, the primary production domain (www.abgupta.me and the earlier abgishere.vercel.app and abgupta.vercel.app redirect to it). The canonical one is set in config (`SITE_URL`, else Vercel's primary production domain), not in code.

## Users

Mixed general audience — no single dominant persona. Recruiters/hiring managers, fellow developers, collaborators, and casual visitors (friends, social shares) all land on the same link and self-select what they explore (resume, dev log, projects, contact).

## Product Purpose

The portfolio's job is (1) get the visitor to view real work (resume, projects, dev log) and take a next step, and (2) be memorable/impressive as an experience in itself — through a continuous, scroll-driven cinematic narrative in the spirit of `why.zero.university`: each scroll increment reveals/unwraps the next beat rather than the visitor paging through static sections.

## Positioning

The differentiator: a single continuous scroll experience where every scroll delta is staged as a reveal — content, motion, and transitions choreographed as one narrative arc, not sections stacked on a page. The rebuild is underway: the atmosphere/background layer shipped first (`0.1.x`), the camera starts moving with scroll in `0.2.x`, and together they set the mood the later content beats are staged against.

## Operating Context

Visitor arrives via a shared link with no prior context and the entire experience unfolds through scrolling. Today the camera pulls back from the mountains, descends onto a desk in a meadow, then turns toward a house on a hill and ends over its terrace pool. The sun and desk lamp switch day and night; the lamp can also be dragged. The device screen and contact form are still to come. Whether an admin surface is wanted at all is undecided.

## Product Principles

- Every scroll increment should feel staged and earned — a deliberate reveal, not a static section boundary — this is the core craft bar for the new direction.
- Content surfaces (Resume, DevLog, Projects) carry real, finalized claims — treat them with the same accuracy bar as a real resume/CV, not marketing copy.
